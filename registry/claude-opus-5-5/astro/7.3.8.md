## astro@7.3.8
- Do not import `z` from 'astro:content' (deprecated, removed in Astro 8). Use `import { z } from 'astro/zod'`; keep `defineCollection` and `reference` from 'astro:content'.
- Zod string-format methods `z.string().email()` and `z.string().url()` are deprecated. Use top-level `z.email()` and `z.url()` instead, e.g. `z.email().optional()`.
- `z.string({ required_error: ... })` no longer compiles: `required_error` does not exist in `$ZodStringParams`. Do not pass `required_error` to `z.string()`.
