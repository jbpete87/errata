## ai@7.0.137
- result.toUIMessageStreamResponse() is deprecated in ai@7; use standalone toUIMessageStream and createUIMessageStreamResponse from 'ai' with result.stream.
- generateObject and streamObject are deprecated; use generateText or streamText with an `output` setting instead.
- result.response is deprecated; use result.finalStep.response instead.
- In ai@7, `totalUsage` on the generation result is deprecated; read `usage` instead (type `LanguageModelUsage`).
- `toUIMessageStream` takes an options object, not a bare stream: call `toUIMessageStream({ stream: result.stream, tools? })`; `result.stream` is `AsyncIterableStream<TextStreamPart<TOOLS>>`.
