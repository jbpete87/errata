## ai@7.0.137
- `generateObject` and `streamObject` are deprecated in ai@7. Use `generateText` with an `output` setting instead of `generateObject`, and `streamText` with an `output` setting instead of `streamObject`.
- `result.toUIMessageStreamResponse()` is deprecated. Use the standalone `toUIMessageStream` and `createUIMessageStreamResponse` helpers from `'ai'` with `result.stream` instead.
- `result.response` is deprecated. Use `finalStep.response` instead.
- In ai@7, `result.totalUsage` is deprecated. Use `result.usage` instead, e.g. `const usage = await result.usage;`.
