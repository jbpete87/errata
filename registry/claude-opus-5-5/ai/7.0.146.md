## ai@7.0.146
- In ai@7, result.toUIMessageStreamResponse() is deprecated; use standalone toUIMessageStream and createUIMessageStreamResponse from 'ai' with result.stream instead.
- In ai@7, generateObject and streamObject are deprecated; use generateText or streamText with an `output` setting instead.
- In ai@7, result.response is deprecated; use result.finalStep.response instead.
- toUIMessageStream takes an options object, not a stream: call toUIMessageStream({ stream: result.stream, tools? }), not toUIMessageStream(result.stream).
- totalUsage on the streamText/generateText result is deprecated; use usage instead (PromiseLike<LanguageModelUsage>).
