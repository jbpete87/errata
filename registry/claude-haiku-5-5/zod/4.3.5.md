## zod@4.3.5
- `required_error` and `invalid_type_error` do not exist on `z.string()`. Pass `error` (a string or error map) or `message` instead.
- `z.string().email()` is deprecated. Use top-level `z.email()`, whose params still accept `message`.
- `z.string().url()` is deprecated. Use top-level `z.url()`.
- `z.string().uuid()` is deprecated. Use top-level `z.uuid()`.
- `z.string().datetime()` is deprecated. Use `z.iso.datetime()`.
- `z.nativeEnum(X)` is deprecated. Use `z.enum(X)` with the enum object.
- `.passthrough()` is deprecated. Use `z.looseObject()` or `.loose()`.
- `ZodString` has no `.ip()` method in zod 4.3.5. The evidence shows no replacement, so do not call it.
