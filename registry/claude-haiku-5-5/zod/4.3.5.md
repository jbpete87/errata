## zod@4.3.5
- z.string() params no longer accept required_error or invalid_type_error; only `error` (string or $ZodErrorMap) and `message` are allowed.
- String format methods .email(), .url(), .uuid(), .datetime() on ZodString are deprecated; use top-level z.email(), z.url(), z.uuid(), z.iso.datetime() instead.
- z.nativeEnum() is deprecated; pass the TypeScript enum to z.enum() instead.
- ZodObject .passthrough() is deprecated; use .loose() or z.looseObject() instead.
- ZodString has no .ip() method; z.string().ip({ version }) no longer exists.
