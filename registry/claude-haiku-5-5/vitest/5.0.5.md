## vitest@5.0.5
- `minWorkers` is not a property of `InlineConfig` in `defineConfig`; use `maxWorkers`, which the compiler suggests.
- `test.skipIf(cond)` returns a collector with signature `(name, options?, fn?)`; pass options before the callback, not after it.
- Do not call expect(...).toThrowError(...), which is deprecated in vitest@5.0.5; use expect(...).toThrow(...) instead.
