## astro@7.4.1
- Import `z` from 'astro/zod', not 'astro:content'; the 'astro:content' re-export is deprecated and will be removed in Astro 8.
- Zod 4 string format methods are deprecated: use top-level `z.url()` instead of `z.string().url()`, and `z.email()` instead of `z.string().email()`.
- `z.string({ required_error })` no longer works; `required_error` is not a property of `$ZodStringParams`.
