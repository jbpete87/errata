## astro@7.4.1
- Deprecated: importing `z` from 'astro:content'. Import `z` from 'astro/zod' instead.
- Deprecated: `z.string().url()` and `z.string().email()`. Use the top-level `z.url()` and `z.email()` instead.
- `required_error` is not a valid option for `z.string()`. Its params type `$ZodStringParams` has no such property; drop it and use the accepted params shape.
